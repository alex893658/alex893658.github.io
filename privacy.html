Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

This explains what personal information TDDC Schools & College ("TDDC", "we", "us") collects through this website and the school portal, why we collect it, and what your rights are. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Who is responsible for your data

TDDC Schools & College, 6-8/43-47 Gideon Street, Agaye Ije-ododo Island, via Ijegun, Ikotun, Lagos State. Email: info@tddcschool.com. Phone: 0817 866 4999.

What we collect

  • Enquiry form: your name, phone number, email (optional), the section and mode you are asking about, and your message.
  • Student portal: admission number, name, class, term results, and fee records (fees charged, payments made, receipt numbers). Parents or students may also upload a photo or PDF of a payment receipt.
  • Staff portal: staff ID, name, role, assigned classes, notes posted for classes, results entered, and payslip records.
  • School finance records: payroll, fee payments, expenses and budgets, which are used only by authorised school administrators and the bursar.

Children's records belong to the school's normal admissions and academic administration. Parents or guardians can ask us about their child's records at any time.

Why we use it

  • To reply to admission enquiries.
  • To run the school: show results and class notes, track fees, confirm payments, and pay staff.
  • To keep the portal secure and prevent misuse.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. The portal keeps you logged in only while the page is open. Closing or refreshing the page logs you out, and you are also logged out after a period of inactivity.

Who handles your data for us

  • Google (Google Sheets, Google Drive and Apps Script) stores the school's records and uploaded receipts.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website pages.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts used on these pages, which means your browser contacts Google when a page loads.

These providers may process data on servers outside Nigeria. We choose providers that protect data to a reasonable standard. We do not share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How we protect it

Portal passwords are stored in hashed form, not as readable text. Logins are limited after repeated wrong attempts. Each person can only see their own records, and staff can only see their assigned classes. Uploaded receipts are kept private and are visible only to authorised finance staff. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private and tell us if you think someone else knows it.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for as long as needed to follow up on admission, then deleted or anonymised. Student academic and fee records are kept for as long as the child is enrolled and for a reasonable period afterwards, as the school and the law require. Financial records are kept for the period required for accounting purposes.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the personal information we hold about you or your child, correct it, delete it where we no longer need it, or stop using it for a particular purpose. Email info@tddcschool.com and we will reply within a reasonable time. If you are not happy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
